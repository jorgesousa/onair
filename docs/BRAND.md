# ONAIR — brand

## Vision

**Just TV.** No ads. No watermarks. No accounts. No tracking.
**Free and open source. Built like it isn't.**

Free software has a reputation: it works, but it looks like nobody cared how. Paid apps have the opposite problem: they look good and are full of ads, upsells, logins and trackers. ONAIR refuses both.

It is a TV player that does only that, and is built to the standard of the best paid software on the Mac: real type, a real colour system, motion with a purpose, every state designed. The code is open so anyone can check the promises, and the quality is there so nobody has to choose between free and good.

What that means in practice:

1. **Nothing between you and the picture.** No splash screen, no sign-in, no "rate us", no upgrade prompt, no watermark. The app opens on your channels.
2. **Nothing leaves the Mac** except the requests to your own provider and one daily check for a new version, which carries nothing about you and can be switched off. No analytics and no crash reporters that phone home.
3. **Quality is not a paid feature.** Every screen gets designed: loading, empty, errors, the about panel. If it ships, it's finished.
4. **Open means checkable.** MIT licence. Every promise here can be verified in the code.

## Lines

- Primary: **Just TV.**
- Promise: No ads. No watermarks. No accounts. No tracking.
- Stance: **Free and open source. Built like it isn't.**
- Short sign-off: Open source. No compromise.

## Voice

- Short. Declarative. Full stops. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype words.
- Say what it does, never what it "empowers" you to do.
- Errors are facts plus the next step: "No signal. Try again."
- Labels are caps and few: LIVE, TUNING IN, ALL, SAVED, RECENT.

## World: Test Card

The app is a broadcast monitor: black and white. Colour appears only where the signal is, as the seven bars.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0E0E10` | Everything dark. TV is watched in dim rooms. |
| Raised | `#18181B` | Fields and hover. |
| Paper | `#F2F0EA` | Text, and the row that is on air (inverted: paper fill, ink text). |
| Live | `#E8332A` | The LIVE pill only. Never used for errors. |
| Bars | `#F2F0EA` `#F5D90A` `#25D6E5` `#2BD44E` `#E83CC8` `#E8332A` `#2A3BE8` | The mark, the tuning card, programme progress, the selected filter. Nothing else. |

- **Type:** Archivo Black (SIL OFL, bundled) for names, labels and statements, in caps. Monospaced for channel numbers and times. System text for programme titles and body.
- **Mark:** the seven bars with the reversed strip under them, and ONAIR in Archivo Black. In the app, the bars alone, as a small tile.
- **Tuning:** a full test card with the channel name on a paper plate, the way a station shows a card while it switches.
- **Motion:** quick springs, no wobble. The on-air row slides to the new channel; the picture fades in over the test card. Reduce Motion turns it all off.

## Never

Ads. Sponsored rows. Upgrade prompts. Watermarks. Splash screens. Telemetry. Colour used as decoration outside the bars.
