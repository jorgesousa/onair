A station ident when you zap in full screen

In full screen the rail and the now bar are gone, so ↑↓ changes channel with no sign of where you landed. A small card top left shows the number, name and what's on, then leaves after 2.5 s. Zapping fast keeps the card up and only the number ticks.

It's the same card the TV window already uses, so the Mac and the TV say the same thing.

02

The test card switches off into the picture

Today the test card fades out over the picture, so for a third of a second you see both at once. Instead, the card collapses to a bright line, like an old set, and the picture comes up behind it. It says "signal acquired" in the brand's own language, in 260 ms.

Purpose
Preventing a jarring change
Frequency
Every channel change, so it stays under 300 ms and never waits on itself
Where
ContentView.swift stage · Motion.swift TuningView
TuningView(channel: c)
  .transition(.asymmetric(
    insertion: .opacity,
    removal: .modifier(
      active: CRTOff(amount: 1),   // scaleY 0.012, brightness +0.8
      identity: CRTOff(amount: 0))))
// removal: .timingCurve(0.55, 0, 1, 0.45, duration: 0.26)
PlayerView(...)
  .opacity(playback.isTuning ? 0 : 1)
  .animation(Motion.fade.delay(0.12), value: playback.isTuning)

The picture visibly moves to the TV

With SHOW ON TV, today the Mac's picture is just replaced by a card. Make the move readable: the Mac picture shrinks and slides toward the TV, the TV switches on from a line, and the station ident lands on it. BACK TO MAC plays it in reverse.

Purpose
Spatial consistency
Frequency
Occasional, a few times an evening, so it can take half a second

05

The first list arrives row by row

After "Tuning in", 269 channels appear in one block. On the first load of a playlist only, let the visible rows rise in with a 30 ms stagger. It's the moment the app comes alive, it's rare, and the list can be clicked while it settles.

Purpose
Delight, at the rare tier
Frequency
Rare. Never on filters, search or relaunch
Where

What should stay still

Considered and turned down. The first is an animation the player has today that should go.

The on-air highlight sliding on ↑↓RemoveIt's keyboard-led and repeated all evening, so the spring makes zapping feel a step behind. Keep the slide when you click a row, and jump instantly on the arrow keys.
Static noise or a CRT effect on every channel changeFrequencyFun the first time, tiring by the tenth zap. Proposal 02 gives the brand moment in 260 ms instead.
Animating the list while you type a searchFrequencyRows moving on every keystroke make the results harder to read.
Moving the guide progress bars in the railFunctionThey're data you scan, so they update in place once a minute.
Hover lift on channel rowsFrequencyThe instant raised background is the right amount for something you pass over constantly.

Beyond motion

Not animation, but they'd make zapping feel like a real TV.

Type a number to zap

Typing 0, 1, 5 jumps to channel 015, like a remote. The ident from 01 shows the digits as you type them.

Back to the last channel

One key, for example ⌫, flips between the two most recent channels, the button every remote has.

What's next, everywhere

The "Next" line from 04 also on hover in the rail, so you can see what's coming without leaving what you're watching.

Verdict

The player is already close to right. Motion is centralised in four tokens, every custom button presses, and Reduce Motion is respected. It needs a little more motion, not much. The biggest win is 01, the full-screen ident, because it fixes a real problem: zapping with no feedback. 02 and 03 bring the Test Card brand into the moments that matter, and removing the slide on ↑↓ makes zapping faster.
