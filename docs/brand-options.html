ONAIR: six ways to be on air

Each direction comes from a real piece of television: the test card, the studio tally light, the set-top box display, broadcast blue. Every one carries the same promise: just TV, with no ads, no watermarks, no accounts and no tracking. Pick one, or combine parts of two.

01 · Test Card

ONAIR

The colour bars are the image everyone knows from television. The app itself stays black and white; the seven bars show only on the mark, the loading screen and the thin progress lines.

InkPaperBars

Type: Archivo Black. Selection is inverted to paper, so colour stays special. Risk: the bars could feel retro if used too often.

02 · Tally

ONAIR

The lit sign outside a studio: warm graphite and one hot red. The channel on air gets a red outline, like the tally light on the camera that is live. The most literal of the six, so it reads instantly.

GraphiteRaisedPaperTally

Type: Unbounded. Red here means "live", not "error": errors take a neutral style. Risk: red sign icons are common.

03 · Broadcast Blue

ONAIR

Inspired by the blue of a TV with no input. The whole app is blue with white type, and the channel on air turns solid white. The mark is a filled O and a bar: "on", or a power switch. No one else in the Dock looks like this.

BlueDeepScreenWhite

Type: Bricolage Grotesque. Square corners. Risk: a coloured app chrome is unusual on the Mac, so the blue has to be dark enough to watch beside at night.

04 · Set-Top

ONAIR

The glowing cyan display on a cable box or a 90s VCR: "ON 01". Channel numbers are the main element, and changing channel flickers the display like real hardware. Warm nostalgia, but sharp.

BlackGlassGridPhosphor

Type: VT323 for numbers and names, a plain sans for programme titles. Risk: a dot-matrix face is hard to read for long text, so keep it to short labels.

05 · Broadside

ONAIR

Swiss-poster type: a light background, very heavy black lettering and one red-orange dot. A light theme when every other video app is dark. The video sits in a black frame, so the room still goes dark around the picture.

StockSheetBlackDot

Type: Schibsted Grotesk Black. Square corners, no radius. Risk: a light sidebar is bright at night, so it needs a dark variant.

06 · Prime Time

ONAIR

Loud and confident, like a late-night channel ident: hot pink on plum-black, tall condensed capitals. Channel names in big type like on-screen graphics. The most daring of the six and the least "tech".

PlumLineBlushPink

Type: Big Shoulders Display. Risk: polarising, which suits a project that wants to stand out.
